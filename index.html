FAQ

Can't I just check their sites myself?

For one or two competitors, you can. It gets hard with 5 brands and hundreds of products across flavors and sizes, because the moves that matter (a stack going 30% off, a popular flavor selling out) are easy to miss on any given day. The free report is a quick way to see what you'd have missed.

There are apps for this already, some for $49 a month or free. Why pay more?

Self-serve tools like Prisync, or free Shopify apps like Snoopie, are good at sending price-change alerts. If someone on your team has time to run one, they may be all you need. You still decide which products matter, match them to yours, and figure out what it means. We do that work for you: a person reviews the data, and you get a written summary instead of a stream of alerts.

You're based in Brazil?

Yes. Targetcode is a data and software development company based in Brazil, and this is one of the data services we run. You get invoices from a registered company in USD and can pay by ACH or card. Our hours overlap almost fully with US Eastern time, and everything runs by email.

Is this legal? How do you collect the data?

We read only public product pages, the same ones any shopper sees, without logging in, at a slow rate, following each site's robots.txt. We collect facts like price and whether a flavor is in stock; we don't copy descriptions, photos or label content. We're not lawyers, so if your team has a policy on this, we'll follow it.

How accurate is the data?

Prices and stock status come straight from each competitor's public product data, as a shopper would see them at the time of the daily check. We report in stock or sold out, not units left, and checkout-only coupons and subscribe-and-save prices aren't visible. If a check fails, the gap is marked as missing. We never fill it in.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel by email. The trial is free with no card, and if your first paid month isn't useful, we refund it. The data you've received stays yours.
